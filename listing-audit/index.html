02What the score covers

Six things, weighted by what moves bookings.

Reviews carry the most weight, then photos and the description. Every check below is part of the score you get.

201020102515
  1. 20

    Photos

    How many photos there are, and how many carry a caption.

  2. 10

    Title

    How much of the 50 characters it uses, whether the area is named, and whether it reads clean or shouts in capitals and emoji.

  3. 20

    Description

    Length, line breaks, the selling points it names, and how strong the first line is.

  4. 10

    Amenities

    The six filters guests use most (wifi, kitchen, washer, workspace, TV, parking) and the total listed.

  5. 25

    Reviews and host

    Average rating, number of reviews and Superhost status.

  6. 15

    Booking setup

    Instant Book, the minimum stay and the cancellation policy.

Scores are guidance based on hosting best practice, not the platforms’ official ranking. Nobody outside Airbnb has that.

03What we usually find

The same four things, again and again.

01

Uncaptioned photos

Guests skim captions, and the platform reads them to understand the listing. One line each: what the space is, and why it is good.

02

Half a title

Airbnb allows 50 characters and many titles use barely half, with the area name buried in the description where nobody searches.

03

Missing amenities

Hosts often leave boxes unticked. Each one is a search filter the villa never appears in, however good it is.

04

A minimum stay that hides you

A long minimum stay filters the villa out of shorter searches. Outside high season it often costs more than it protects.
