02Three leaks

Commission is only the loss you can see.

01

The fee you can see

Between 3% and 25% of every booking, depending on the platform, your fee setup and the market. On a villa grossing six figures, that is a salary leaving every year.

02

The rate the algorithm sets

Smart pricing tools are tuned for the platform’s booking volume, not your yield, so peak dates often go for less than the calendar could hold.

03

The guest you never own

Every repeat stay booked through the platform pays commission again. Without the guest’s email, you buy the same guest back year after year.

03What we actually do

Direct bookings, without losing the platforms.

  • Keep the booking sites working. They stay switched on for discovery. We stop them owning every repeat guest.
  • A direct booking engine. Your own site and calendar, with no platform commission on the booking.
  • Own the guest list. Post stay campaigns and referral offers bring guests back without paying to reach them twice.
  • Pricing set for your yield. Seasons, weekends and events priced for your revenue, not the platform’s volume.
