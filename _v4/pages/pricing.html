10FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Which plan is right for my villa?

Growth suits most villas with 5 or more bedrooms, and smaller villas that already perform well. Choose Excellence if your villa already earns around $90K a year or more and you want more of your bookings to come to you directly.

How is the fee set?

Each plan is one flat yearly fee, quoted for your villa, with setup included. You have the quote before you start.

Why a flat fee and not a percentage?

We don't handle your money, so there's nothing to take a cut of. You keep every booking in full, and the fee stays the same when your revenue grows.

Do you replace my villa manager or staff?

No. Your team keeps running arrivals, cleaning and maintenance. We work on what decides the rate guests pay and whether they find you.

Where do the property analytics come from?

AirDNA data for entire home listings in Bali with 6 or more bedrooms, available 271 to 365 nights a year and booked at least one night, over the 12 months to October 2026. “Top 10%” is the level nine in ten properties of that size don't reach.

I have a 1 or 2 bedroom property.

Start with the Revenue Gameplan. At that size a yearly plan would cost close to what a full management company charges, so the Gameplan is the better value. Smaller listings that already perform near the top can still qualify for Growth, so if yours already beats the market, let's talk.

I own several villas. How is that priced?

Your highest priced villa pays its plan in full and each other villa is 25% off. Villas can be on different plans and can share one brand and booking website. Depending on your portfolio, we will make an offer that suits your needs: tell us about your villas in the free audit form.

I manage villas for other owners.

With 6 or more villas we agree partner pricing for the whole portfolio. Tell us how many villas you run and their sizes.

Can I cancel?

Results take time, so each plan has a minimum term: 4 months for Growth and 12 months for Excellence. After that, you can cancel any time.
